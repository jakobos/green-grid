# C010 · The export is one A4 sheet at 300 dpi with a fit-to-page map and a scale bar — outcome

Every PNG export is now exactly 2480 × 3508 px (portrait) or 3508 × 2480 px (landscape), and `downloadPng` uses pixel ratio 1. `EXPORT_PIXEL_RATIO` and the old pixel layout constants are gone. The layout math lives as pure, tested functions in `src/export/sheetLayout.ts`: `sheetSizePx`, `mapAreaMm`, `pickScale` (1:10 … 1:1000), `chooseOrientation` (larger scale wins, ties go to portrait), `gridStepMeters`, `labelStepMeters` and `scaleBarLengthMeters`. The final values are a 12 mm margin, a 28 mm title block, 10 mm and 8 mm label gutters (side and top), a 60 mm legend column, and `LEGEND_CONTENT_GAP_MM = 8`. The gap is taken inside the legend column, not from the map area, so 100 × 10 m stays landscape at 1:500. Grid lines run every 1 m unless 1 m is under 3 mm on paper (then 5 m, then 10 m). Labels use a multiple of the grid step and sit at least 8 mm apart, for example 1:200 gives 1 m lines and 2 m labels.

Stroke widths are now set in millimetres (grid 0.1, boundary and scale bar 0.45, items 0.3, compass 0.5), and the hachure gap is 2 mm. Text sizes are in points, 8 pt at the smallest. Roughness values are unchanged from C007, so at 300 dpi the wobble looks subtler than in the old small PNG. At 1:10 no round length falls in the 40–60 mm range, so the scale bar falls back to 1 m drawn 100 mm long. The metre-label box width (10 mm) is a literal in `drawDimensionLabels`, while the gap test uses `LABEL_SIDE_MM`; keep the two in step. The map is centred vertically, so wide gardens leave empty paper under the title, which slice 6 may use. Item name labels overflow small items; slice 5 replaces them with markers.
